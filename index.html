About AutoTech MSJA

AutoTech MSJA has been serving the Greater Montreal area since 2015, providing honest, reliable automotive repair and maintenance services to drivers who value quality workmanship and transparent pricing. We're a locally-owned shop that treats every vehicle like it's our own.

Our Story

Founded by master technician Marc-Simon Jean-Antoine, our shop was built on a simple principle: automotive service shouldn't be complicated or intimidating. After years of working at dealerships and corporate chains, Marc-Simon saw too many customers being oversold services they didn't need or left confused about what was actually wrong with their vehicles.

AutoTech MSJA was created to be different. We explain repairs in plain language, show you what needs fixing, and give you options that fit your budget and timeline. No pressure, no upselling—just straight talk about what your vehicle needs.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Certified Expertise: Our technicians hold ASE and provincial certifications, with ongoing training on the latest vehicle technologies
  • Diagnostic Precision: We invest in professional-grade diagnostic equipment to identify problems accurately the first time
  • Transparent Pricing: You'll receive a detailed written estimate before any work begins, with no surprise charges
  • Local Commitment: We're your neighbors, and we're invested in building long-term relationships, not one-time transactions

Our Team

Our three certified technicians bring over 40 combined years of automotive experience. We work on all makes and models, from domestic vehicles to European and Asian imports. Whether you drive a 2005 Honda Civic or a 2023 Tesla, we have the knowledge and equipment to service it properly.

Visit us at our shop on Boulevard des Sources in Pointe-Claire, or call (514) 555-0147 to discuss your vehicle's needs. We're here Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM, and Saturdays until 4 PM.
